\begin{abstract}
Let $N(T,2T)$ count nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function with
$T<\gamma\le 2T$, with multiplicity, and let $N_0^s(T,2T)$ count the simple
zeros on the critical line.  We give machine-certified finite data which,
through the arbitrary-window Gabor interface of Anthropic and the stability
refinement of \texttt{ainta/zeta-simple-zeros}, imply
\[
\liminfT\frac{N_0^s(T,2T)}{N(T,2T)}
\ge 0.6732001170127618568\ldots
>\frac{1683}{2500}.
\]
This improves the preceding $0.673137630699\ldots$ construction.  The new
finite ingredient is a retuned position-weighted seven-point gap inequality,
proved by exhaustive interval subdivision, combined with the existing
re-optimized window and sharp square-root Gram-defect profile.  The finite
inequality, window estimates, span capacities, and final arithmetic are
certified with Arb.  The new assembly has not been formalized end to end in
Lean, so the result is presented as a research-draft record candidate pending
independent mathematical review.
\end{abstract}

Two independent same-day repositories, located after the finite certificate
and initial draft were complete, report candidates
$0.673192911473\ldots$ and $0.673195198901\ldots$
\cite{Tawan2026,Pipitone2026}.  The present value is numerically higher than
the latter by $0.0000049181112412813\ldots$ in proportion.  Neither artifact
was an input to this construction, and the numerical comparison establishes
neither accepted priority nor independent validity.

\section{Statement and proof boundary}

\begin{theorem}[certified reduction]\label{thm:main}
Assume the arbitrary-admissible-window asymptotic interface stated in
\cite[\S7.1]{Claude2026} and the stability and shifted-block interfaces of
\cite{Ainta2026}, with their stated uniformity.  Then
\[
\liminfT\frac{N_0^s(T,2T)}{N(T,2T)}
\ge 0.6732001170127618568\ldots
>\frac{1683}{2500}.
\]
\end{theorem}

The hypothesis in Theorem~\ref{thm:main} is a proof boundary, not a numerical
assumption.  The cited works supply those analytic statements; this
repository certifies every new finite assertion needed to instantiate them.
Unlike the headline theorems in the Anthropic Lean artifact, however, this
particular window, the stability refinement, and the square-root assembly
have not yet been joined in one formal proof.

The framework of \cite{Claude2026,Ainta2026} uses Weil's explicit formula on
a Gabor family of windowed test functions.  It produces a real symmetric
matrix $\widetilde G$ whose zero side splits into a positive-semidefinite part
from on-line zeros and signature-$(1,1)$ blocks from off-line pairs.
Montgomery's first and second moments evaluate $\tr\widetilde G$ and
$\|\widetilde G\|_F^2$.  The refinement of \cite{Ainta2026} retains a
stability defect $\tr\Psi(M)$, where $M$ is the Gram matrix of the simple-zero
atoms and
\[
\Psi(t)=
\begin{cases}
(t-1)^2,&0\le t\le2,\\
2t-3,&t\ge2.
\end{cases}
\]
We import the explicit-formula and trace asymptotics, the tail estimates, and
the compact-uniform Gram-entry asymptotic from \cite{Claude2026}; from
\cite{Ainta2026} we import the stability rank--trace inequality,
convex pinching, and shifted-block averaging.

\section{The window}\label{sec:window}

For an even profile $v\ge0$ on $[-\tfrac12,\tfrac12]$ (the square of the
taper, before the standard boundary ramp), define
\[
c_1(v)=\frac{(\int v)^2}
{\int v^2+\iint|s-s'|v(s)v(s')\,ds\,ds'},
\qquad H(v)=2-\frac1{c_1(v)},
\]
and the normalized overlap kernel
\[
k_v(x)=\frac{K_v(x)}{K_v(0)},\qquad
K_v(x)=\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}v(t)\cos(2\pi xt)\,dt.
\]
The Montgomery--Taylor profile maximizes $H$ alone.  The defect term below
responds at first order to a profile perturbation while the loss in $H$ is
second order, creating a small net improvement.

We use $v(s)=\sum_{j=0}^{6}c_j\cos(\omega_js)$ with
\[
\omega=(\sqrt2,2\pi,4\pi,6\pi,8\pi,10\pi,12\pi)
\]
and exact rational coefficients
\[
c=\frac1{10^9}
(10^9,3322500,-7609135,1190194,-731476,-1680572,1141360).
\]

\begin{proposition}[certified]\label{prop:window}
On $[-\tfrac12,\tfrac12]$,
\[
\frac34\le v\le1,
\qquad \frac{v'(s)}s<-0.7763\quad(0<s\le\tfrac12),
\]
and
\[
H(v)=0.6724570414145442887\ldots
\ge H_{\rm cert}:=\frac{3362285207}{5000000000}.
\]
In particular, $v$ is nonincreasing on $[0,\tfrac12]$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Arb interval evaluation on an $8192$-cell subdivision proves the range and
monotonicity bounds.  At the removable zero,
\[
\frac{v'(s)}s=-\sum_jc_j\omega_j^2\operatorname{sinc}(\omega_js),
\qquad \operatorname{sinc}(z)=\frac{\sin z}{z}.
\]
The functional is evaluated from the closed forms
\[
\int v=\sum_jc_j\operatorname{sinc}(\omega_j/2),
\quad
\int\cos(as)\cos(bs)\,ds=\frac12\left[
\operatorname{sinc}\frac{a-b}{2}+\operatorname{sinc}\frac{a+b}{2}
\right],
\]
and the corresponding exact expression for
$\iint|s-t|\cos(as)\cos(bt)\,ds\,dt$.  The recorded Arb enclosure is
\texttt{certificates/fast-parts.txt}.
\end{proof}

Because $v$ is positive and nonincreasing, $\sqrt v$ followed by the fixed
outward-monotone boundary ramp of \cite[\S2.2]{Claude2026} has total variation
at most $2$; the same holds for its square.  Positivity $v\ge3/4$, the finite
trigonometric expansion, and the fixed ramp give the required first- and
second-derivative bounds with a profile-dependent constant.  The fourth
moment condition also holds eventually: away from the $O(1)$ ramp,
$\int v^2\ge9/16>1/2$.  Thus the cited arbitrary-window interface gives, for
$S=N_0^s(T,2T)$ and $N=N(T,2T)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:interface}
S\ge H(v)N+\tr\Psi(M)-o(N).
\end{equation}
Writing an explicit Lean \texttt{AdmWindow} instance remains future work.

\section{A sharp square-root tail for the block defect}

Write $w=k_v^2$.  For a positive-semidefinite block $G$ set
\[
E=2\sum_{i<j}|G_{ij}|^2,
\qquad \Delta=\tr\Psi(G).
\]

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:sqrt}
$\Delta\ge h(E)$, where
\[
h(E)=
\begin{cases}
E,&0\le E\le1,\\
2\sqrt E-1,&E\ge1.
\end{cases}
\]
The profile is sharp.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use $\Psi(t)=(t-1)^2-(t-2)_+^2$.  For the eigenvalues $\lambda_i\ge0$ put
$X=\sum_i(\lambda_i-2)_+^2$.  Then
\[
E\le\sum_i(\lambda_i-1)^2=\Delta+X.
\]
If $X=0$, then $\Delta\ge E$.  If $X>0$, each $\lambda_i>2$ contributes
$1+2z_i$ to $\Delta$, where $z_i=(\lambda_i-2)_+$.  Consequently
$\sum_i z_i\le(\Delta-1)/2$ and
$X\le(\sum_i z_i)^2\le(\Delta-1)^2/4$.  Thus
$E\le(\Delta+1)^2/4$, so $\Delta\ge2\sqrt E-1$; moreover
$\Delta\ge1\ge E$ when $E\le1$.  Sharpness follows from one eigenvalue
$a>2$ and $n-1$ equal eigenvalues $(n-a)/(n-1)$, conjugated to constant
diagonal, and then letting $n\to\infty$.
\end{proof}

Since $h$ is increasing and concave with $h(0)=0$, for every $A>0$ and
$\eta=h(A)/A$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:chord}
h(E)\ge\eta\min(E,A).
\end{equation}

\section{The certified seven-point inequality}\label{sec:certificate}

For gaps $g_1,\ldots,g_6\ge0$ between seven consecutive simple zeros put
$y_0=0$ and $y_j=g_1+\cdots+g_j$.  Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:F}
F(g)=\frac1{2736}\sum_{i=1}^{6}g_i
+\sum_{0\le i<j\le6}a_{ij}w(y_j-y_i).
\end{equation}
The exact numerators $10^6a_{ij}$ are
\[
\setlength{\arraycolsep}{10pt}
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
i\backslash j&1&2&3&4&5&6\\ \hline
0&236484&535224&969656&1000000&1000000&2000000\\
1&&381281&464776&30344&0&1000000\\
2&&&382235&0&30344&1000000\\
3&&&&382235&464776&969656\\
4&&&&&381281&535224\\
5&&&&&&236484
\end{array}
\]
and are reflection symmetric.  Direct rational summation gives, for every
span $1\le r\le6$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:capacity}
\sum_{i=0}^{6-r}a_{i,i+r}=2.
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[certified]\label{prop:F}
For every $g\in[0,\infty)^6$,
\[
F(g)\ge\frac{891}{200000}=0.004455.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
An exhaustive interval subdivision with grid $1/4000$ visited
$2{,}168{,}370$ boxes and certified every one, reaching maximum depth $50$.
Arb constructs the kernel and derivative tables; all conversions to binary64
are rounded outward.  The branch-and-bound proof uses pressure, direct
interval, and convex-tangent lower bounds.  Near removable singularities in
the sinc derivatives, it switches to exact-sign rational branch tests and
alternating series with explicit tail bounds.  The complete transcript and
table hashes are in
\texttt{certificates/retuned-p2736-grid4000.txt}.  The observed floating-point
minimum $0.0044623605595\ldots$ is used only as a discovery diagnostic; it is
about $0.16\%$ above the certified target.
\end{proof}

\section{Deduction of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}}

Set
\[
q=6,\quad m=272,\quad \varepsilon=\frac{891}{200000},\quad
p=\frac1{2736},\quad B_p=qp=\frac1{456}.
\]

\emph{Step 1 (window sum).}
For a block $B$ of $m$ consecutive simple zeros, sum \eqref{eq:F} over its
$m-q$ consecutive seven-point windows.  A pair spanning $r$ gaps receives
total weight at most $2$ by \eqref{eq:capacity}, and each gap appears in at
most $q$ windows.  With $E_B=2\sum_{i<j}w(y_j-y_i)$ and
$A:=\varepsilon(m-q)=1.18503$, this gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:block-energy}
E_B+B_p\operatorname{span}(B)\ge A.
\end{equation}

\emph{Step 2 (bounded-span case split).}
Put
\[
R=h(A)=2\sqrt A-1=1.177181664446\ldots,
\qquad \eta=\frac RA=0.993377099690\ldots.
\]
If $B_p\operatorname{span}(B)\ge A$, then
$\eta B_p\operatorname{span}(B)\ge R$, so the pressure term alone wins.
Otherwise
$\operatorname{span}(B)<A/B_p$, a fixed constant.  The compact-uniform
Gram-kernel asymptotic therefore applies simultaneously to all pairs in the
fixed-size block, and the Gram off-diagonal energy equals $E_B-o(1)$
uniformly.  Equations \eqref{eq:chord} and \eqref{eq:block-energy} then yield
\begin{equation}\label{eq:block-defect}
\tr\Psi(G_B)+\eta B_p\operatorname{span}(B)\ge R-o(1).
\end{equation}
Indeed, if $E_B\ge A$ the defect supplies $R-o(1)$; otherwise the two terms
supply at least $\eta(E_B+B_p\operatorname{span}(B))-o(1)$.

\emph{Step 3 (averaging).}
Remove the standard endpoint strips of normalized width $L^2$ in the
mesoscopic notation of \cite{Ainta2026}; their contribution is $o(N)$.
Convex spectral pinching gives
$\tr\Psi(M)\ge\sum_B\tr\Psi(G_B)$ for disjoint blocks.  Average
\eqref{eq:block-defect} over the $m$ shifted block partitions.  Every
interior gap is charged in at most $m-1$ offsets, the total normalized span
is $N+o(N)$, and leftover endpoint blocks contain only $O(m)$ points per
offset.  Therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:average}
\tr\Psi(M)\ge
\frac RmS-\eta B_p\frac{m-1}{m}N-o(N).
\end{equation}

\emph{Step 4 (assembly).}
Insert \eqref{eq:average} into \eqref{eq:interface}, use
$H(v)\ge H_{\rm cert}$, and rearrange:
\[
\frac SN\ge
\frac{272H_{\rm cert}-\eta(1/456)271}{272-R}-o(1)
=0.6732001170127618568\ldots
>\frac{1683}{2500}.
\]
The final Arb enclosure has width below $10^{-50}$ and is recorded in
\texttt{certificates/fast-parts.txt}.  Replacing the data by the preceding
$67.313763\%$ design reproduces its value, while the separate gate reproduces
the \cite{Ainta2026} seven-point certificate.

\begin{remark}
This optimization does not offer a route to $100\%$.  The obstruction for
certificates using only the bandwidth-one data in the Anthropic Lean artifact
is $0.68182868746\ldots$, while the subsequent Campaign~2 analysis gives the
narrower pure pair-energy class a ceiling near $0.674826$.  A $100\%$ liminf
result would require qualitatively stronger information; even then it would
establish a density-one statement, not rule out every exceptional zero.
\end{remark}

\section{Provenance}\label{app:provenance}

This repository revision starts from commit \texttt{e0a2266} of
\cite{Trmdy2026}.  That work supplied the perturbed window, square-root
profile, generalized verifier, and the preceding $67.313763\%$ design; its
multi-model contribution record is preserved in \texttt{docs/provenance.md}.
The present follow-up was initiated and directed by Vivaswat Ojha in a
ChatGPT Work conversation with OpenAI Codex.  It keeps the window fixed and
retunes the pressure, exact pair weights, and block length.  Codex carried out
the numerical search, implementation, certificate construction, verifier
hardening, and much of the initial drafting; Ojha is the human author and
takes responsibility for submitting this contributor draft.  The upstream
maintainer is invited to determine how any merged or published version should
be organized and attributed, without disturbing this contribution record.
The revision also makes the bounded-span case split and endpoint bookkeeping explicit.
Numerical optimization was used only to discover a rational candidate.  The
exhaustive interval certificate is the decision procedure.
