# Status and literature — density-one on the critical line

- **Established results (pre-this-preprint)**: Hardy (1914) infinitely many critical zeros; Selberg (1942) positive proportion; Levinson (1974) ≥ 1/3; Conrey (1989) > 2/5; Pratt–Robles–Zaharescu–Zeindler (2020) > 5/12; Alpöge–Furman (2026, per the paper's [1], arXiv 2608.13637) > 2/3 for simple critical zeros.
- **Our project's record**: C₉(ζ) = 0.673066472675939665848… (k=9, f₉=0.00392 certified) — a quantitative lower bound on the proportion, *conditional* on the T1/T2/T3 bridge in the general-k framework (Stage C in progress).
- **Zenodo 22008814 claim**: unconditional `N0(T)/N(T) → 1` (density one). If correct, it supersedes the entire positive-proportion line and our record trivially.
- **Prior art overlap**: the preprint's references include Goldston–Suriajaya (critical-line zeros), Arya–? (Landau–Gonek), and modern moments literature — overlapping our project's literature maps.
- **Novelty status**: density one is a long-standing open conjecture; no peer-reviewed proof is known. This is a very recent, non-peer-reviewed preprint; its claim is extraordinary and needs independent verification.
